We've taught enough lessons to know one thing with certainty: every group has the same characters in it. The cautious one, the show-off, the one who definitely lied on their booking form about being intermediate, the one already drinking at 11am.

We can't prove this is astrologically determined. But we've spent a long time on the slopes, and we've seen things. So, lightheartedly and with our tongues firmly in cheek, here's your ski horoscope for 2026.

## Aries (21 March – 19 April)

You're the one who skis off before the instructor has finished the safety briefing. You consider blue runs an insult. You're going to ski faster than you can technically handle, and you're going to enjoy every minute of it.
**Resort:** Val d'Isère — the terrain matches your ambition.

## Taurus (20 April – 20 May)

You spent six months researching this trip. You have a spreadsheet. You know exactly which restaurant you're eating at on Wednesday. You're going to have an excellent week because you have prepared for it like a small military operation.
**Resort:** Méribel — premium quality, plannable, dependable.

## Gemini (21 May – 20 June)

You changed your mind about which group lesson to join three times. You're already chatting to a stranger on the chairlift. You will ski every piste once and master none of them, and you'll have a brilliant time anyway.
**Resort:** Three Valleys — too many options is exactly right for you.

## Cancer (21 June – 22 July)

You booked the chalet specifically because it had a hot tub. You're the one organising the group dinner. Your idea of a perfect ski day involves two runs, a long lunch, and an afternoon nap.
**Resort:** Morzine — cosy, sociable, doesn't push too hard.

## Leo (23 July – 22 August)

You bought new ski kit specifically for this trip. You have opinions about your goggles. You will be photographed at the top of every notable peak. You ski better than you think you do, but only marginally.
**Resort:** Verbier — finally, a resort that takes itself as seriously as you do.

## Virgo (23 August – 22 September)

You've done the YouTube tutorials. You know the technical name for what you're doing wrong. You're frustrated that your parallel turns aren't perfectly symmetrical yet. You will, however, improve faster than anyone else in the group.
**Resort:** St Anton — technical terrain rewards your perfectionism.

## Libra (23 September – 22 October)

You wanted everyone to agree on the resort, which is why you're still deciding in November. You ski beautifully when relaxed and badly when anxious. You're the diplomat who smooths things over after Aries cuts up Capricorn on the chairlift queue.
**Resort:** Les Gets — pretty, balanced, no extremes.

## Scorpio (23 October – 21 November)

You're suspiciously good at skiing and won't tell anyone how often you actually go. You disappear off-piste at 10am and come back at 4pm with mysterious tales. You make the rest of the group feel inadequate.
**Resort:** La Grave — of course it's La Grave.

## Sagittarius (22 November – 21 December)

You're the one suggesting heli-skiing, ski touring, and "have we considered Kazakhstan?" Your ski holiday is really an expedition. You will exhaust everyone you ski with, and you will be entirely unaware of this.
**Resort:** Chamonix — the only place that can keep up with you.

## Capricorn (22 December – 19 January)

You ski with quiet competence and zero fanfare. You've been going to the same resort for twelve years. You don't see the point in changing what works. You ski better than the show-offs, and you know it.
**Resort:** Zermatt — classic, reliable, no need to prove anything.

## Aquarius (20 January – 18 February)

You're the one wearing a strange retro one-piece ironically. You found a tiny resort no one's heard of and won't stop talking about it. You'll spend an hour explaining why everyone else is doing skiing wrong.
**Resort:** Engelberg — niche enough to satisfy you, big enough to ski seriously.

## Pisces (19 February – 20 March)

You're skiing today because someone else booked the trip. You're not entirely sure what level you're at. You will, however, fall in love with the mountains in a profound way and become slightly insufferable about it for the next year.
**Resort:** Saas-Fee — beautiful, gentle enough to find your feet, dramatic enough to convert you.
