## The job

Peak's marketing runs on real footage of real instructors teaching real
clients. No stock, no staged handshakes, no anonymous skier in a jacket. That
means someone has to be out on the hill with a camera, most days, all season.

That is this job. You shoot in the morning, cut in the afternoon, and publish
while it is still true. A powder morning in Avoriaz should be online that
evening, not in a content calendar for March.

## What you'd be making

Short-form video is the bulk of it - lesson moments, coaching explainers,
resort conditions, the GAP course cohort's season. Alongside that you keep the
photo library alive: instructor portraits shot to a consistent crop, resort
imagery through the season, lesson photography we can actually use on the
site.

You will also write. Our journal is a real SEO engine, not a blog nobody
reads, and you will publish into it.

## Creative control

We have a defined brand and a house voice, and inside it you have genuine
room. If you think we are shooting the wrong thing, say so - the last two
changes to how we film lessons both came from the person holding the camera.
