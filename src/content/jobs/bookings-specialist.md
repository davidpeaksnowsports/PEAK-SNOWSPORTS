## The job

Every good week at Peak starts as a well-built schedule. This is the person
who builds it.

You are the first voice a client hears and the one who decides which
instructor they get. That second part matters more than it sounds - matching a
nervous adult beginner to the right coach is the difference between a client
who never skis again and one who books the same instructor for six seasons.

## A normal day

Mornings are the inbox and the day's schedule - who is teaching what, who
moved, what the weather has broken. Afternoons are next week: building the
grid, chasing confirmations, sorting the awkward requests. Saturdays in season
are transfer day, arrivals day and the busiest day of the week.

You will work in SkiOperator, our own booking platform, which means when
something about it is wrong you tell the people who build it and it gets
fixed. That is a rare thing in this industry.

## Who you'd work with

You sit with the bookings manager and alongside the resort directors, talking
to instructors all day. Small team, no layers, decisions made in the room.
