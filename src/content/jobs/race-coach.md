## The job

Peak's race programme is growing fast and we want someone to own the coaching
side of it. This is gates, video, timing and the unglamorous early mornings
that make a season's progress possible.

You will run training blocks for our club squads, take race camps through
holiday weeks, and coach private race clients who want serious technical work.
Across a season you should see the same athletes enough times to genuinely
develop them, which is the point.

## What makes this different

Most seasonal race coaching jobs are a fortnight of camps bolted onto a
teaching diary. This is not that. The race programme is a product in its own
right, with its own calendar, and you are the person shaping it.

You will also have a hand in the pathway - spotting the strong twelve-year-old
in a Kids Club group and getting them into the right squad is as much a part
of the role as setting a course.

## Who you'll be coaching

Juniors from first gates through to genuine club racers, plus adults who want
to be technically better than they are. Parents are engaged, invested and will
ask you real questions. We back coaches who answer them honestly.

Send the form below. We read every application and reply to all of them. For
this role expect a technical conversation with our head of coaching, and if
you are in the Alps, a session on snow together.
