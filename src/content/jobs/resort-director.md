## The job

A Peak resort director runs their resort. The team, the teaching standard, the
relationships in the valley and the season's numbers all sit with you.

The hardest part is not the schedule. It is holding a standard - having the
conversation with an instructor whose lessons have gone flat, and having it
early and well enough that they get better instead of leaving.

## What you own

Your instructor team is yours: who you recruit, how you brief them, how they
develop. You have the final call on safety and terrain in your resort. You
work with bookings on the schedule rather than receiving it. And you carry
your resort's commercial performance, with the numbers shared openly and the
authority to do something about them.

## The team around you

You join a small leadership group - the other resort directors, the bookings
manager, and the founders. It meets often, argues honestly and moves fast.
Peak is British-owned and family-run; there is no head office three countries
away.

## Where it leads

Peak is building beyond the ski school - the GAP course, ski camps, and
SkiOperator, the booking platform we build ourselves. People who run a resort
well here end up shaping those too.
